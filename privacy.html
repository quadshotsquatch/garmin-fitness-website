Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Overview

Garmin Fitness Insights is a personal fitness analytics application developed for private, individual use. It collects fitness and wellness information to generate personalized reports and interactive dashboards.

2. Information Collected

The application may process Garmin activity records, heart rate measurements, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep information, Body Battery measurements, training metrics, and related wellness data. Activity records may contain location information.

3. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used to analyze fitness trends, monitor training and recovery, and generate personalized reports and dashboards.

4. Data Storage

Garmin fitness information is processed through a private GitHub Actions workflow and stored in the application developer's Google Drive account. Fitness records are not published on this website.

5. Google Drive Access

Google Drive authorization is used to create and update application-generated fitness reports and data files in the developer's Google Drive account. The application requests the Google Drive file scope and does not require access to Gmail or other Google services.

6. Data Sharing

Fitness information is not sold or publicly distributed. Data may be shared with third-party services selected by the developer, such as an AI assistant, when the developer chooses to submit information for analysis.

7. Data Security

API credentials and authorization tokens are stored using private configuration and GitHub Actions secrets. Access to fitness records is controlled through the developer's accounts and service permissions.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Fitness reports and data files are retained until deleted by the developer. Files can be removed directly from Google Drive, and access to connected services can be revoked through the relevant account settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.

10. Contact

Questions about this application or its privacy practices can be directed to the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
